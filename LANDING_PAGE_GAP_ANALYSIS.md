## WHAT CURRENTLY WORKS
- The opportunity-led hero line creates the category in one sentence.
- The emotional opening (Truth → Journey → Invisible Economy) builds belief before logic — exactly right.
- The canvas Network is the signature visual and must remain the centre of gravity.
- Progressive enhancement + reliability (loads with no libraries) — a luxury signal in this market.
- The operational app is genuinely interactive and real — the strongest proof asset we have.

## WHAT SHOULD NEVER BE REMOVED
- The hero category line and its cinematic treatment.
- The canvas Network (Ch04) and the global finale.
- The Invisible Economy chapter — it is the only place economic participation currently lives in the brand story.
- The Farmstead proof layers inside `pulse-os-v2.html` (all 27 pages).
- The two-product separation (brand site ≠ app). Do not merge them.

## WHAT STRENGTHENS THE MOAT (and must be amplified)
- **Verified Participation Intelligence** is the moat. The brand site currently *implies* it (Network chapter) but never *names and demonstrates* it. Naming it is the single biggest moat-strengthening opportunity.
- **Trust Intelligence** — the verified-discovery formula proves recommendations are earned, not bought. It lives in the app but is absent from the brand narrative.
- **Ask PULSE** — querying the network ("What opportunities exist around Sani Pass?") is the most vivid demonstration that PULSE is *intelligence infrastructure*, not software. It is the missing centrepiece.

## WHAT WEAKENS THE STORY (today)
- Discovery (Ch05) shows *magic* but not *why it's trustworthy* — a sophisticated buyer (tourism board, investor) will ask "how do you know these recommendations are real?" The trust answer is missing from the narrative.
- The scale chapter ends at "Global Network" as an abstraction — there is no concrete **destination-authority** moment (a tourism board / province seeing its economy). This caps perceived market size.
- There is no proof bridge. Vision without visible proof reads as a pitch deck. Belief needs "...and it's running today."

## WHAT FEELS TOO SaaS
- Nothing egregious. The "Meet PULSE" capability chips edge toward a feature list — keep them, but ensure they are framed as *intelligence layers*, not features. The roadmap (if added) must be a journey, never feature cards.

## WHAT FEELS TOO HOSPITALITY-FOCUSED
- The story currently resolves at the property/guest level. For destination-scale belief it must visibly extend to **municipalities, tourism boards, provinces, nations, and economic participation** — present only faintly today. This is the difference between "hospitality software" and "destination operating layer."

## WHAT LIMITS PERCEIVED MARKET SIZE
- Absence of a **Destination Command** moment. Without showing a tourism board / city / country view, the addressable market reads as "accommodation operators" (thousands) rather than "destinations and economies" (a category).
- The roadmap's Phase 5–7 (Destination / Opportunity / Sovereign Intelligence) is invisible on the brand site.

## WHAT LIMITS FUTURE SCALABILITY
- The narrative does not yet articulate the **participation graph as a compounding asset** — that every interaction makes the network more valuable. Without this, PULSE looks linear, not exponential.

## WHAT WEAKENS CATEGORY CREATION
- PULSE is described in places via what it does (operations, discovery) rather than what it *is* (infrastructure / operating layer / network). The category-defining frame ("not software — infrastructure") needs an explicit, unmissable moment.
- "Why Now?" is absent. Categories are created by naming the inflection (fragmentation, review overload, search fatigue, AI noise, trust collapse) and positioning PULSE as the resolution.

## WHAT PREVENTS EMOTIONAL CONVICTION
- The arc currently lands on "Why This Matters" + finale, but never delivers the **proof beat** ("it's real, here it is"). Conviction = Vision (belief) + Proof (trust). Proof is missing.

## WHAT PREVENTS DESTINATION-SCALE POSITIONING
- No cinematic destination/economic command centre.
- No explicit statement that the same intelligence runs a cottage and a country.

## WHAT OPPORTUNITIES ARE MISSING (the build list)
Ranked by moat + category impact:

1. **Ask PULSE — Intelligence Interface (NEW Ch05.5, centrepiece).** Bloomberg/Palantir-grade query surface with authoritative, visual, interactive outputs. Demonstrates PULSE = intelligence infrastructure. *Highest category-creation value.*
2. **Verified Discovery / Trust moment (NEW chapter).** The formula: Google Discovery + Owner Validation + Guest Feedback + Trust Signals = Verified Discovery. Names the trust moat. *Highest moat value.*
3. **Farmstead Live — Proof bridge (NEW Ch).** A cinematic "this is running today" section that surfaces real readiness / reviews / discovery / trust scores and links directly into the live operational app (`pulse-os-v2.html`). Converts vision into trust. *Highest conviction value.*
4. **Destination Command (NEW Ch).** Cinematic tourism-board/province/nation command centre — visitor flows, sentiment, trust scores, opportunity clusters, economic participation. *Highest market-size value.*
5. **Every Destination Has Hidden Opportunity (NEW Ch).** Signals → Trust → Intelligence → Opportunity flow connecting guest…municipality…tourism board…community. *Category + moat.*
6. **Why Now? (NEW Ch).** Fragmentation / review overload / search fatigue / AI noise / trust collapse → "PULSE connects everything." *Category creation.*
7. **The Roadmap as a Journey (NEW Ch).** 7 intelligence layers (Hospitality Ops → Trust → Growth → Local Ecosystem → Destination → Opportunity → Sovereign) revealed as an ascending journey, not cards. *Scalability narrative.*

### Build constraints
- All new chapters reuse the existing design system, motion engine (GSAP/ScrollTrigger/Lenis + canvas), `.reveal-init` pattern, and counters — no new dependencies.
- AI invisible: outputs branded "PULSE Insight / PULSE Recommendation / PULSE Opportunity" only.
- Ask PULSE must be interactive (clickable prompts cycle authoritative visual answers) but require no backend — pre-composed, evidence-styled responses.
- Farmstead Live links into `pulse-os-v2.html` (real pages) — it proves, it does not duplicate.
- Reduced-motion + reliability preserved throughout.

### Final-test gate (must pass before "complete")
Would a guest / owner / hotel group / tourism board / municipality / investor / government stakeholder believe it? The new Destination Command + Farmstead Live + Ask PULSE chapters are specifically what make the answer "yes" for the institutional audiences (board / municipality / investor / government) who currently have nothing to believe in on the brand site.
