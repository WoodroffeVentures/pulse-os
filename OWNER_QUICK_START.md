# PULSE OS — Owner Quick Start

## Sign In

1. Go to **https://pulse-os-steel.vercel.app**
2. Click **Owner Login →** (top right or footer)
3. Sign in with your production email

The sidebar will show your organisation name. If it shows "No organisation", contact the platform administrator to link your account.

---

## Set Up Properties

1. Click **Properties** in the sidebar (OPERATIONS section)
2. Click **+ Add Property**
3. Fill in the property name, type, city and check-in/check-out times
4. Click **Save as Draft** (you can publish later)

Add all four Farmstead properties:
- Woody's Self Catering Cottage
- Swallows Nest Self Catering Studio
- Meadows Cottage
- Jackals Rest

---

## Create a Test Reservation

1. Click **Reservations** in the sidebar
2. Click **+ New Reservation**
3. Select a property, enter guest name and dates
4. Click **Confirm**

The reservation appears in the calendar. Overlapping confirmed reservations for the same property are automatically rejected.

---

## Run the PULSE Core Workflow

**Step 1 — Business Profile**
- Click **Businesses** → **+ Add Business**
- Fill in name, category, district, contact details
- Set Evidence State to "Self-entered"
- Save

**Step 2 — Create an Opportunity**
- Click **Opportunities** → **+ Create Opportunity**
- Fill in title, type, geography, target audience
- Set status to Draft

**Step 3 — Viability Assessment**
- Click **Viability**
- Select your business and opportunity from the dropdowns
- Click **Run Assessment**
- Review score, confidence (will be Low with self-entered evidence), recommendation
- Click **Save Assessment**

**Step 4 — Record a Decision**
- Click **Record decision →** on the saved assessment, or go to **Participation & JVs**
- Choose your decision: Join / Join with Conditions / Hold / Not Suitable
- Add conditions and milestones if applicable
- Save

**Step 5 — Complete a Milestone**
- In Participation & JVs, expand your record
- Click **Mark Complete** next to a milestone

**Step 6 — Record an Outcome**
- Click **Outcomes**
- Find your participation record → click **+ Outcome**
- Describe what was achieved
- Choose Self-reported or Evidence-confirmed
- Save

**Step 7 — View the Graph**
- Click **Participation Graph**
- Expand your record to see the full chain:
  Business → Opportunity → Assessment → Decision → Milestones → Outcomes

## Generate a Pilot Report

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar
2. Click **Download HTML** next to "Controlled Pilot Report"
3. Opens in browser — use Print → Save as PDF

---

## Check System Status

- Click **Settings** to see live system status
- Shows: Database, Authentication, Storage, Google integration, AI engine, Email status

## Sign Out

Click **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar.
