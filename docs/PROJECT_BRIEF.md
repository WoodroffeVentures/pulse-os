# PULSE OS Project Brief

## Product Vision

PULSE OS is an opportunity participation intelligence platform. The short-term product is PULSE Hospitality OS: a daily operating system for real accommodation operators. The long-term platform becomes the verified infrastructure layer that can answer:

- What is the opportunity?
- Who should participate?
- Is the business operationally and commercially ready?
- What outcome is expected?
- What action must happen next?
- What measurable result occurred?

The long-term moat is the Verified Opportunity Participation Graph: a relational, evidence-based model of businesses, assets, opportunities, readiness, participation and measured outcomes.

## Production MVP

The production MVP is Farmstead Hospitality, one hospitality business on one farmstead with four accommodation units:

- Woody's Self Catering Cottage
- Swallows Nest Self Catering Studio
- Meadows Cottage
- Jackals Rest

Farmstead is the only live tenant in the MVP. All other sectors, destinations, government surfaces and opportunity engines are architecture-ready but locked.

## Phase 1 Live Scope

Build these as operational modules:

- Command dashboard
- Portfolio dashboard
- Properties and property detail
- Bookings calendar
- iCal sync registry
- Tasks
- Housekeeping
- Maintenance
- Works register
- Guest CRM
- Reviews
- Guest guides
- Brain / knowledge base
- AI daily brief
- AI recommendations
- Settings
- Import/export bridge for offline HTML data

## Roadmap Only

The following modules must remain visible but locked:

- Phase 2: Hospitality Visibility Intelligence
- Phase 3: Hospitality Growth Intelligence
- Phase 4: Local Ecosystem Intelligence
- Phase 5: Destination Intelligence
- Phase 6: Opportunity Participation Intelligence
- Phase 7: Economic / Government Intelligence

Locked modules may have static demo dashboards and narrative cards. They must not ship live workflows, automated decisions, or tenant-facing production claims.

## Success Criteria

- Farmstead staff can see arrivals, departures, cleaning, maintenance, reviews, guests and knowledge in one operating interface.
- All operational data is tenant-scoped and ready for Supabase RLS.
- AI output is draft-first, logged and governed.
- iCal feed sync has a real data model and placeholder function boundary.
- The product visibly supports future tourism, travel, hospitality group and government expansion without implementing those workflows yet.
