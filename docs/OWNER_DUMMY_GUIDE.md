# PULSE OS — Owner Guide
*Written for a non-technical operator. No prior technical knowledge assumed.*

---

## What is PULSE OS?

PULSE OS connects your accommodation property, local businesses and guests in one place. It helps you:
- See all your bookings, tasks and reviews in one dashboard
- Receive and respond to opportunity pitches from local businesses
- Track joint-venture partnerships with local suppliers
- Share destination intelligence with the tourism board

---

## How to access the platform

Open your web browser and go to:
- **Dashboard:** your-domain.vercel.app/dashboard
- **Board Presentation:** your-domain.vercel.app/board-demo
- **Business Onboarding:** your-domain.vercel.app/onboarding
- **Guest Lite (share with guests):** your-domain.vercel.app/guest

You will receive a magic link by email to sign in — no password to remember.

## Signing in

1. Go to the platform URL
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your inbox for a link from PULSE — click it
4. You are now signed in

## Understanding the Opportunities Inbox

Local businesses can pitch opportunities to your property — offering their services free of charge in return for a share of revenue generated.

When a pitch arrives:
1. Go to **Opportunities** → **Venue Inbox**
2. Review the pitch and its viability score (out of 100)
3. Choose one of four responses:
   - **Accept** — opens a joint-venture account; revenue is tracked automatically
   - **Decline** — records the decision with a reason
   - **Accept with Conditions** — sets specific requirements the business must meet first
   - **Request Presentation** — asks the business to present their case in person

All decisions are recorded automatically. Nothing is sent to the business until you confirm.

---

## What does the viability score mean?

The score (0–100) measures how likely an opportunity is to succeed based on verified evidence:
- **75–100** — Strong match. Recommended to proceed.
- **55–74** — Possible. Review the evidence gaps.
- **40–54** — Conditional. Specific requirements must be met first.
- **Below 40** — Not recommended based on current evidence.

The score is calculated automatically from public information and documents the business has submitted. It is not a guarantee of success.

## How to invite your team

Go to **Settings → Team** → enter their email address → assign a role:
- **Manager** — full access to all modules
- **Front Desk** — bookings, guests, tasks
- **Housekeeping** — housekeeping schedules and task completion
- **Maintenance** — maintenance requests and work items

They will receive a magic link to join.

---

## What is DEMO DATA vs LIVE data?

Any screen showing **⬛ DEMO DATA** contains fictional example information used for demonstration and training purposes only. It is clearly labelled and cannot be confused with real business data.

Screens showing **● LIVE** contain real data from your connected accounts.

---

## What the platform does NOT do automatically

- Send messages on your behalf without your approval
- Accept or decline opportunities without your decision
- Make financial transfers or payments
- Share your guests' personal information with third parties
- Record, store or analyse CCTV footage

Every AI suggestion requires your approval before any action is taken.

---

## Getting help

For platform support, contact the PULSE team. For tourism board queries, contact your designated board officer.

If something looks wrong, click **Report Issue** at the bottom of any screen, or send a description to your PULSE contact.

---

## Costs to be aware of

The following features have usage costs based on how much you use them:
- **Google Places lookups** — charged per API call (cent amounts; budget alerts configured)
- **AI suggestions** — charged per AI request (cent amounts; monthly budget set)
- **File storage** — charged per GB stored (minimal for most properties)

Everything else is included in your platform subscription.
