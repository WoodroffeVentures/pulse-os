# PULSE OS — Southern Drakensberg Pilot Proposition
*For tourism board consideration. 2026-08-08. All demo data clearly labelled.*

---

## The Problem Being Tested

Tourism spend in the Southern Drakensberg is fragmented. Visitors lack verified, connected local discovery. Local businesses — restaurants, guides, artisans, wellness providers, experience operators — have no structured, evidence-based channel to participate in tourism activation. Tourism boards have no live, evidence-graded view of which businesses participated in which opportunities, under what conditions, with what measured outcome.

**The question PULSE tests:** Can a structured, verified Opportunity Participation Network — connecting venues, local businesses and guests — increase measurable local economic participation during a 90-day pilot?

| Group | Role | Expected number |
|---|---|---|
| Tourism Board / DMO | Governance, access to board dashboard | 1–3 officers |
| Accommodation properties | Venue that receives and decides on pitches | 6–10 initially |
| Local businesses | Opportunists who pitch services FOC | 15–25 initially |
| Community enterprises | Artisans, cooperatives, community guides | 5–10 initially |
| PULSE team | Platform, onboarding, support | 2–3 |

---

## Proposed Initial Geography

- Underberg (hub)
- Himeville
- Sani Pass corridor
- Nottingham Road (secondary)

Rationale: accessible cluster with existing accommodation and a mix of established and emerging local businesses. Sani Pass provides a distinct, high-demand anchor experience.

## 90-Day Phase Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–30)
- Tourism board signs pilot agreement
- PULSE onboards 6–10 accommodation properties with verified profiles
- PULSE onboards 15–20 local businesses with evidence submission
- Verification process for all profiles
- First 5 opportunities created and scored
- Tourism board dashboard live (initially demo data, migrating to real as profiles complete)

### Phase 2 — Activation (Days 31–60)
- First opportunity decisions by venues (accept / conditional / decline / present)
- First JV account activations
- Guest discovery layer live for Drakensberg region
- Weekly board check-in on dashboard metrics
- First readiness actions completed by conditional participants

### Phase 3 — Measurement (Days 61–90)
- All active JV accounts tracked with revenue and milestones
- Outcome evidence collected for completed activations
- Participation Graph entries logged
- Board presentation of measured outcomes vs baseline
- Pilot continuation decision by board

## Data Required from the Board and Participants

From the tourism board:
- List of known accommodation properties in the pilot geography
- Any existing registered business databases (CIPC / TETA / local register) for matching
- Any existing visitor survey or intent data for baseline comparison
- Governance contacts for data-sharing agreement

From participants:
- Business registration or identity verification
- Category, location, operating hours, accessibility information
- Portfolio, brochure or capability documents (optional but improves viability score)
- Consent to participate and how their data will be used

PULSE provides:
- Profile templates and guided onboarding (non-technical)
- Evidence submission via photo, document upload or web link
- Verification workflow and audit trail

---

## Governance and Ownership

- Source code, schema and all data remain owned by the operator / PULSE — not a cloud-locked vendor
- Tourism board has read access to aggregate destination data only (individual business data visible only to the business and authorised PULSE staff)
- No individual guest data is shared with the board without explicit consent
- All AI outputs are draft-first — no autonomous public action
- Breach and escalation placeholders in place; legal review required before production data processing
- POPIA-aligned purpose limitation and consent recording are included in the design; final legal sign-off is the board's responsibility

---

## Onboarding and Training for Non-Technical Users

- No app download required — mobile web link
- Step-by-step guided profile creation (photograph your sign, describe your service)
- Plain South African English throughout
- One primary action per screen
- WhatsApp-based update notifications (manual trigger, not automated)
- Tourism board gets a 2-hour walkthrough session + recorded guide

## Success Measures (90 Days)

| Measure | Target | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Verified profiles | 20 | PULSE dashboard count |
| Opportunities created | 10 | PULSE pipeline |
| Venues deciding (any response) | 80% of pitched venues | Response log |
| JV accounts opened | 5 | PULSE account log |
| Readiness actions completed | At least 3 conditional participants completing required action | Evidence audit |
| Outcome evidence completeness | ≥80% of activated JVs have tracked revenue | JV account data |
| Community / SME inclusion | ≥30% of participants are micro or community enterprises | Profile category |

**Not measured in this pilot:** GDP impact, jobs created, visitor spend, causal tourism recovery. These require methodology and data beyond a 90-day pilot.

---

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low venue uptake (venues don't respond to pitches) | Start with 3 early-adopter venues committed before launch |
| Low business profile completion (evidence gaps) | Guided onboarding with PULSE support; FOC assistance for first 10 profiles |
| Sani Pass seasonality limiting participation | Plan activation windows for May–September peak; flag infrastructure constraint in dashboard |
| Data quality too low for meaningful board reporting | Set 65% evidence completeness as minimum threshold before a profile appears in board view |
| Legal / POPIA uncertainty | Operate as data processor under board's authority; consent flows and purpose limitation designed in; legal review before any personal data stored in production |
| AI unavailability | All scoring is deterministic (no AI dependency); AI is explanation layer only; system degrades gracefully |

## Decision Requested from the Board

1. **Confirm pilot participation** — Tourism board officer as named governance contact
2. **Identify 3–5 early-adopter accommodation venues** willing to receive and respond to opportunity pitches during Phase 1
3. **Identify 5–10 local businesses or community enterprises** willing to create verified profiles and submit a pilot pitch
4. **Data-sharing agreement** — minimal: what PULSE may aggregate and present in the destination dashboard
5. **Proceed or pause decision** at 90 days based on measured participation outcomes
