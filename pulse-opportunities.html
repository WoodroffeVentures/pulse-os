02 — How It Works

From pitch to joint venture
in four steps.

01
Opportunist Pitches
Create a structured offer: what you provide, which venue, and what JV terms. Free to pitch. PULSE scores viability before the venue sees it.
›
02
Venue Reviews
The venue sees the opportunity in their inbox: viability score, proposer profile, evidence, and proposed JV terms. They control the next action entirely.
›
03
JV Account Opens
On acceptance, PULSE opens a shared JV account. Revenue flows in and splits automatically per the agreed terms. Both parties see every transaction.
›
04
Outcomes Tracked
Revenue, milestones and outcomes are recorded to the Opportunity Graph — improving future viability scores for this opportunist and similar venues.
