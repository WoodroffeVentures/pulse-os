## WHAT WE ARE BUILDING
PULSE OS is **the world's first Opportunity Intelligence Network** — an intelligence layer connecting guests, properties, businesses, experiences, communities, destinations, tourism, investors, government, economic development, participation, opportunity and impact.

PULSE OS is **NOT** (these are merely components, never the identity): a hospitality platform · PMS · guest app · booking engine · review platform · tourism website · marketplace · AI assistant · dashboard application.

## THE CORE PROBLEM
The world operates in fragments — businesses, destinations, communities, government, investors and guests all in silos. Everyone sees pieces; nobody sees the ecosystem. So opportunities are missed, businesses stay invisible, tourism underperforms, partnerships never happen, communities lose value, governments lack visibility, investors miss viable projects.

## THE PULSE THESIS
Every destination contains hidden opportunity. Most people never see it. **PULSE discovers it · verifies it · activates it · measures it.**

## THE END STATE
Not hospitality software. A **Global Opportunity Intelligence Network** where every business, property, destination, activity, guide, restaurant, event, municipality, tourism board, investor, supplier and community participant becomes part of a living intelligence graph.

## THE INTELLIGENCE MODEL (must appear everywhere)
```
Signal → Verification → Trust → Participation → Outcome → Intelligence → Opportunity
```

## THE TRUE MOAT
Not AI, dashboards, reviews, maps, bookings or integrations. The moat is **VERIFIED PARTICIPATION INTELLIGENCE** — the platform learns who participated, what happened, what worked, what failed, what created value, and what should happen next. **Every action strengthens the graph.**

8. **PULSE NETWORK** — the Participation Graph (everything a node, relationship, intelligence)
9. **PULSE INTELLIGENCE** — Ask PULSE (the intelligence layer, never a chatbot)
10. **PULSE IMPACT** — outcome measurement (the government/economic story)
11. **PULSE SOVEREIGN** — national intelligence (long-term vision only — never scoped or built early)

## NON-NEGOTIABLE OPERATING ARTICLES
*(Retained from prior constitution; still binding under the new doctrine.)*

**Art. I — Core Doctrine.** AI Assists · Humans Govern · Evidence Decides · Participation Creates Value · Opportunity Creates Growth · PULSE Connects Everything. No AI output takes a public action without human approval.

**Art. II — Trust Is Earned, Never Purchased.** Trust Scores are computed from evidence. No business may pay to raise a score. Trust scores are **visible internally, never exposed publicly**. The integrity of the Trust layer is the foundation of the entire network.

**Art. III — The Guest's Safety Is Paramount.** Only Approved/Preferred participants reach a guest. PULSE never surfaces a flagged or unvalidated participant.

**Art. IV — Farmstead Proves The Model; It Does Not Define It.** Farmstead = Tenant #001 / Production Environment #001 / proof & validation / showcase. It must remain live and interactive — and must never become the entire story. Nothing may architecturally limit PULSE to Farmstead's context.

**Art. V — The Moat Is The Graph, Not The Features.** Every feature must strengthen Verified Participation Intelligence. "Build the network, not the features."

**Art. VI — No Autonomous Public Actions, Ever.** PULSE may draft, suggest, recommend, classify, alert. It may never autonomously publish, send guest-facing comms, change pricing, cancel bookings, or make legal/financial commitments without explicit human approval.

**Art. VII — Privacy By Design.** Guest data belongs to the guest; tenant data is RLS-isolated and never crosses org boundaries. POPIA / GDPR / sovereign compliance is architecture, not policy.

**Art. VIII — Evolve Through Enhancement, Never Replacement.** PULSE is grown, not rebuilt. Refactor; do not discard working systems. Every session leaves the system stronger.

**Art. IX — Visibility Is Not Validation.** Future domains/verticals may be visible as direction and demo, but must not ship live workflows, automated decisions, or production claims until their phase activates. The demo/production line must always be explicit.

**Art. X — Sovereignty.** Government/municipal deployments keep data within jurisdiction (region isolation, sovereign hosting, air-gap options). No sovereign data passes through a third-party model without consent and a compliant DPA. PULSE SOVEREIGN is vision-only until explicitly authorised.

**Art. XI — AI Invisibility.** Users never see Claude/GPT/Gemini/DeepSeek/Anthropic/OpenAI. Users see only: PULSE Insight · PULSE Recommendation · PULSE Draft · PULSE Alert · PULSE Opportunity. AI is infrastructure; PULSE is the product.

## THE SIX-QUESTION BUILD GATE
Before implementing ANY feature, it must strengthen at least one — ideally several — of:
1. Participation 2. Trust 3. Intelligence 4. Opportunity discovery 5. Economic visibility 6. The graph.
**If it strengthens none → do not build it.**

## THE FINAL TEST
Would a property owner / hotel group / tourism board / municipality / government stakeholder / investor / local business / community partner immediately understand: *What opportunity exists? What is it worth? Who should participate? What action to take? What impact is created?* If not — keep improving.
