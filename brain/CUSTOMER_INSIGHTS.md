### Pain Points (Observed)
1. **WhatsApp group chaos** — Operational coordination happens in WhatsApp. Messages get lost. Urgent tasks compete with casual chat. No accountability tracking.
2. **Paper checklists** — Pre-arrival and checkout checklists exist on paper. No digital record. No photo evidence. No accountability trail.
3. **Review response delays** — Reviews on Booking.com, Airbnb, and Google go unanswered for days because they're checked on different platforms at different times.
4. **Maintenance forgotten** — Small maintenance issues (cabinet hinge, hot water pressure, WiFi signal) are noted verbally and forgotten. No tracking system.
5. **Guest history gaps** — Repeat guests (like Kobus van der Berg, 5 stays) have no centralised record of preferences, special occasions, or past issues.
6. **Revenue visibility** — Total monthly revenue requires adding up numbers from 4 different OTA dashboards.
7. **Booking gaps** — Gap nights are not systematically identified or acted upon until revenue is already lost.

### Adoption Drivers (What will make Nadia use PULSE daily)
1. **Morning Brief replaces WhatsApp morning check** — If the platform tells her everything she needs in one view in under 60 seconds, she'll use it.
2. **AI review responses save time** — Writing review responses is a daily chore. If PULSE drafts responses that she can approve with one tap, she'll use it.
3. **Staff accountability is automatic** — If she can see whether Thandi completed the pre-arrival checklist without calling her, she'll use it.
4. **Revenue gaps surfaced automatically** — If PULSE tells her "Meadows Cottage has 3 gap nights coming — here's a LekkeSlaap promotion draft", she'll use it.

### Adoption Risks
1. **Platform too complex for staff** — If Thandi and Sipho don't adopt the Staff Hub, the operational data layer is empty and the platform fails.
2. **Onboarding friction** — If setup takes more than 30 minutes, Nadia will abandon it. Farmstead's 4 properties and iCal URLs must be pre-seeded.
3. **Offline reliability** — JoyWood Farm has intermittent connectivity. The platform must work offline or degrade gracefully.
4. **Cost sensitivity** — R999/month (Professional) is justifiable given the revenue profile. R1,500+ would face resistance.
