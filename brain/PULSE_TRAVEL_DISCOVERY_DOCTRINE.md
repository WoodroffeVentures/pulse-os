# PULSE TRAVEL & DISCOVERY — DOCTRINE
*Domains 2 (TRAVEL) + 3 (DISCOVERY). The bridge between the guest and the Opportunity Network. Source of truth for the Travel & Discovery experience and the production travel/discovery engines. Extracted from the original Google Places route, the Local Discovery taxonomy, the AI Getaway concept and the Trust formula. Authored 2026-05-31.*

---

## POSITIONING (what it is NOT)
Not a booking engine. Not Expedia / Booking.com / TripAdvisor / Google Travel. Those help people *find products*. **PULSE helps people discover the right opportunity** — understand, participate, experience, connect.

> Traditional platforms help people **book**. PULSE Travel helps people **discover · understand · participate · experience · connect.**

## CORE MISSION
Every destination contains extraordinary experiences. Most visitors never find them; most businesses never benefit; most tourism boards cannot see them. PULSE changes that. **The internet solved access. It did not solve discovery.**

## THE CENTRAL SHIFT
People do not want more options — they want **the right experience, at the right time, in the right place, for the right reason.** Discovery is not about searching; it is about **intelligent participation in a destination ecosystem.**

---

## THE DISCOVERY GRAPH
A destination becomes a **living discovery network** — restaurants, experiences, activities, trails, guides, events, communities, markets, culture, nature, properties — all connected through intelligence. Every place, activity, experience and recommendation is a node.

## VERIFIED DISCOVERY (the differentiator)
Not every recommendation deserves visibility. **Evidence first, recommendations second.** Public signals (from the Google intelligence layer) feed validation:
- Google Business Profile · Maps · Reviews · Images · Operating Hours · Categories · Website signals · Social signals.
Formula (from PULSE TRUST): **Google Discovery + Owner Validation + Guest Feedback + Trust Signals = Verified Discovery.** Only Approved/Preferred surface to guests. *The internet rewards visibility; PULSE rewards trust — trust is the currency of discovery.*

## AI TRAVEL INTELLIGENCE (from the AI Getaway concept)
**Inputs:** Destination · Dates · Budget · Travel Style · Interests · (optional) social profile connection.
**Travel styles:** Romantic · Family · Adventure · Hiking · Wellness · Food & Wine · Nature · Luxury · Business.
**Output:** Accommodation · Activities · Experiences · Dining · Transport · Events · suggested itinerary · estimated spend.
**Doctrine:** PULSE does not generate itineraries — **it generates personalised opportunities.** Draft-first; the guest experiences intelligence before booking.

## BEYOND ACCOMMODATION
Most platforms stop at booking. PULSE continues:
```
Accommodation → Activities → Restaurants → Events → Local Businesses → Community Experiences → Destination Participation
```
**The stay is only the beginning.**

## LOCAL DISCOVERY ENGINE (taxonomy — from the v2 prototype)
Google-Maps-inspired layered ecosystem map. Categories: **Restaurants · Adventure · Family · Nature · Culture · Events · Shopping · Essentials · Guides · Wellness · Hiking · Fishing · Seasonal Experiences.** Each expands dynamically. Every destination becomes searchable; every recommendation validated; every experience measurable.
**Place object (from Google Places route):** name · category · rating · distance_km · trust status.

## DYNAMIC DESTINATION INTELLIGENCE
Destinations constantly change — weather, events, seasonality, demand shifts, search trends, occupancy trends, visitor movement. **Discovery should change with them.**

## LAST-MINUTE OPPORTUNITY ENGINE
Every unfilled room, empty seat and unsold experience is hidden opportunity. PULSE combines low-occupancy property + available activities + restaurant promos + event tickets + transport into a **dynamic opportunity package.** (Bridges directly to PULSE Opportunities / Revenue Gaps.)

## THE BRIDGE TO OPPORTUNITY (critical)
```
Discovery → Participation → Opportunity → Impact
```
Discovery is not the destination — it is the **beginning of participation.** Guest ↔ restaurant ↔ guide ↔ event ↔ property ↔ community: discovery connects them, participation activates value, opportunity creates impact.

## SCALE
Southern Drakensberg (Farmstead pilot) → KwaZulu-Natal → South Africa → Africa → Global Network. **PULSE is not tied to one destination — it is a framework for every destination.**

## FIVE DISCOVERY LENSES (audience value)
- **Guest:** What should I do?
- **Property:** What should I recommend?
- **Tourism Board:** What should we promote?
- **Business:** How do I participate?
- **Investor:** Where is demand emerging?

## PLACE IN THE ECOSYSTEM
HOST runs the operation · **TRAVEL guides the guest · DISCOVERY surfaces the ecosystem** · TRUST validates participation · OPPORTUNITIES activates value · DESTINATION measures regional performance · NETWORK connects everything · INTELLIGENCE learns · IMPACT measures. **Travel & Discovery are the bridge between the guest and the opportunity network** — never standalone products.
