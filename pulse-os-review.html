AI Governance

AI assists. Humans approve. Every action is logged.

AI May
  • Draft social posts
  • Draft review responses
  • Summarize reviews
  • Suggest tasks
  • Generate daily briefs
  • Identify booking gaps
  • Recommend maintenance follow-up
  • Generate guest message drafts
AI May Not
  • Publish publicly without approval
  • Reply to reviews automatically
  • Send guest messages automatically
  • Change pricing automatically
  • Cancel bookings
  • Make legal commitments
  • Make financial commitments
